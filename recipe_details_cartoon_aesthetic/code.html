Creamy Garlic Pasta

A rich, comforting classic that comes together in minutes. Perfect for busy weeknights!

schedule 25 min bolt Easy restaurant 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 oz fettuccine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/4 cup parmesan, grated
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1

    Boil a large pot of salted water. Cook fettuccine according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.

  2. 2

    In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1-2 minutes. Do not let it brown.

  3. 3

    Lower the heat. Stir in the heavy cream and simmer gently for 3 minutes until slightly thickened. Gradually whisk in the parmesan cheese.

  4. 4

    Toss the cooked pasta into the creamy sauce. Add splashes of reserved pasta water as needed to reach desired consistency. Season with salt and pepper.
